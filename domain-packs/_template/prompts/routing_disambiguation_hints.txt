## TODO routing tie-breakers

- TODO collision 1 (e.g. wet-lab vs computational; experimental vs simulation):
  - If the paper's primary independent variable is TODO-A, route to TODO-template-A.
  - If the primary variable is TODO-B, route to TODO-template-B.
  - For mixed cases, prefer the dominant contribution; document the choice in routing_evidence.

- TODO collision 2 (e.g. methods vs performance; description vs argument):
  - If the headline contribution is TODO-A, route to TODO-template-A.
  - If the headline contribution is TODO-B, route to TODO-template-B.
  - The signal: what does the abstract lead with? what do the figures defend?

- TODO collision 3 (e.g. review vs research article; perspective vs roadmap):
  - All TODO-similar genres route to review-or-perspective; the template handles

  - If a document has > X% original data, it's a research article, not a review.
